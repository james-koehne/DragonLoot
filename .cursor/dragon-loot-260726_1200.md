# Dragon Loot

## Dragon Loot

![Image from Dragon Loot](https://app.milanote.com/media/p/images/1WJVSG16J4vN71/GKK/image.png?w=800)

**Little Hoardkeeper**

Count and clean up the dragons treasure.

You are a gnome, playing in first person with physics, within a fantasy cave dungeon, and a giant sleeping dragon shadow lurking in the background, back lit, with huge towering massive piles of loot.

   Pet dragon

     Gnomes associated with treasure / goblin / dobbe / hobbit / cute) - can pick male or female character

Piles of coins and treasures, your task is to count and tidy up, stack and organize them for the dragon. Coins stack cleanly, treasure cleaned / shined up and presented on tables and racks.

    Some treasure locked away in chests? You have to lockpick / open up? Or can find hidden keys in the piles - don't have keys hidden in random places.

    Coin counter mechanism / equipment, weighing station for treasure - tools to determine value

    A few different shapes, colors, styles of coins to group them together.

    Treasure crowns, jewels, cups, etc.

    Finding structures or things under large treasure stacks

    Treasure broken up by sections, limited access initially, clean and stack, complete a section to unlock next section

    Set tables / display cabinets that hold set items - table of gold cups, display of gold helmets etc.

    Is lighting a factor, being able to see what you're doing. Placing light sources? Torches, fireflies, lanterns. Day / night cycle? So during day you get some natural light.

Performance: not all coins / treasure has physics, i.e. treasure in the background is static and you can't reach it until you've cleared the current section.

    Multiple static stacks of treasure that scale down as you take physical treasure off the top

Upgrades; a few basic abilities to make your job easier can be unlocked over time.

    Ability to run. Muffled shoes to not wake dragon. Flight to reach higher points? Helpers? Faster counting. Jumping. Pickup more at one time. Glowing light around you.

Spells; a few unique spells to make job easier

    Highlight spell to find similar items. Ordering / sorting spell. Auto-shine spell. Pickup duplicate object, upgrade to pickup all of type. Auto sorting display cabinets.

Potions, Jewelry items for additional abilities.

Sense of mystery, exploring deeper into the cave. Simple premise, easy to understand, everyone knows to not wake the dragon.

Ending, dragon flies away, you clean up the final treasure pile and build a bed for the dragon who comes back to sleep.

Golden Needle

---

Harry potter music, satisfying to watch shelves fill up. Map initially to learn where things go. 25 Shelves. Satisfying to learn layout. Level up by completing a set of books / completing a shelf to earn skill points, eventually takes more shelves to earn a skill point. Hidden keys to unlock a chest, with a scroll inside that grants a new ability. Spells by skill points. Keys randomly placed didn't feel good. Every upgrade only 1 skill point for simplicity. There's an element of figuring out the book title vs where it should be stacked. Playstyles, no magic vs only magic. Report at end of whole game showing play style. Had to google how you unlock chests / find keys - improve onboarding to how you do stuff. Doesn't have physics for books stacked, when you remove one underneath and top books float - doesn't feel good. The idea of waking the dragon and items potentially moving, prevents things being so static, only slightly move items. Day / night cycle.
